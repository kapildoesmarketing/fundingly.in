Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 19, 2026 • fundingly.in

Welcome to fundingly.in ("Fundingly.in", "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to transparency and respecting your personal privacy while providing free public venture intelligence and startup funding trackers.

1. Information We Collect

fundingly.in is a static, serverless web application that does not require user accounts, logins, or passwords to access.

  • Public Funding Data: All startup funding rounds, founder names, and company details presented on this platform are aggregated from publicly available news sources, press releases, and articles.
  • Anonymous Usage Telemetry: We may collect standard aggregate access logs (such as browser type, referral URLs, and pages visited) to ensure site reliability and performance.
  • Voluntary Newsletter Subscriptions: If you subscribe to our funding alerts, your email address is handled securely via our third-party email provider and used strictly for dispatching curated funding digests.

2. Cookies and Advertising (Google AdSense)

We may partner with third-party advertising networks, including Google AdSense, to serve relevant advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to our website or other websites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

3. Compliance with India DPDP Act

We adhere to applicable data protection principles, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Any collected communication data is retained only as long as necessary to provide requested updates.

4. External Links & Attribution

Our dashboard links directly to source news publishers, press releases, and external websites for fact verification. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, wish to request data correction, or want to report inaccurate public deal data, please reach out to the Fundingly team at hello@fundingly.in or visit our Contact Us page.
