2. FEATURE & UX PILLAR 1: SEAMLESS INTERACTION & DISCOVERY
================================================================================

2.1 Clickable Entity Pivots (Instant Cross-Filtering):
    - Problem: Investor names, sector tags, cities, and stage badges on deal cards and table rows are static text.
    - Actionable Solution:
      * Clicking an Investor Pill (e.g., "Blume Ventures", "Peak XV") immediately filters all deals backed by that investor.
      * Clicking a Sector Badge (e.g., "DeepTech", "D2C", "Fintech") immediately filters by that sector.
      * Clicking a Location (e.g., "Bengaluru", "Mumbai") immediately sets the Geography filter.
      * Clicking a Funding Stage Badge (e.g., "Seed", "Series A") immediately sets the Stage filter.
    - UX Benefit: Transforms the dashboard into an interconnected relational intelligence graph.
    - Effort: ~25 lines of event delegation in `js/app.js`.

2.2 Shareable URL Query State Synchronization (Deep Linking):
    - Problem: Users cannot share filtered views (e.g., "Seed stage fintechs in Bengaluru") on WhatsApp, Twitter, or Slack.
    - Actionable Solution:
      * Two-way sync between active UI filters and browser URL parameters without page reloads using `history.replaceState`:
        `https://fundingly.in/?stage=Seed&sector=Fintech&city=bengaluru`
      * On initial page load, parse `window.location.search` and automatically apply the corresponding filters.
      * Supports standard browser Back and Forward navigation buttons.
    - UX Benefit: Dramatically boosts virality and professional referral traffic on LinkedIn, X, and WhatsApp groups.
    - Effort: ~35 lines of vanilla JS in `js/app.js`.

2.3 Floating Active Filter Chips Bar with 1-Click Reset:
    - Problem: When multiple filters are active across dropdowns and sidebar, users cannot see their active combinations in one glance.
    - Actionable Solution:
      * Render a sticky horizontal pill bar below the toolbar whenever any filter is active:
        [ Investor: Unitary Fund ✕ ] [ Sector: Fintech ✕ ] [ Stage: Series B ✕ ] [ Clear All Filters (3) ]
      * Clicking an individual "✕" removes only that specific filter.
      * Clicking "Clear All" instantly resets all filters to default with zero friction.
    - UX Benefit: Crystal-clear feedback on why certain results appear and effortless filter management.
    - Effort: ~30 lines in `js/app.js` and ~20 lines in `css/styles.css`.

2.4 Interactive Search Quick Suggestions Dropdown:
    - Problem: The search input is a blank field; first-time visitors might not know what to search for.
    - Actionable Solution:
      * When focusing the search bar (`#searchInput` or `⌘K`), display a sleek frosted popover with:
        - 🔥 Trending Sectors (e.g., DeepTech, Quick Commerce, Generative AI, Clean Energy)
        - 💼 Active Investors (e.g., Peak XV, Blume Ventures, Avaana Capital)
        - 🏙️ Top Hubs (e.g., Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, Mumbai)
      * Clicking any suggestion instantly applies the search keyword or filter.
    - UX Benefit: Frictionless onboarding for new visitors and zero-typing discovery.
    - Effort: ~45 lines in `js/app.js`.

2.5 Spotlight-Style Command Palette Modal (⌘K / Ctrl+K):
    - Context: Modern developer & analyst tools (Linear, Raycast, Notion) use centered command palettes.
    - Actionable Solution:
      * Pressing `⌘K` opens a floating blurred modal overlay with an instant action & deal search:
        - Type company name -> Jump straight to company dossier page
        - Type `stage:seed` or `sector:ai` -> Filter feed
        - Quick Actions: `Export CSV`, `Toggle Currency`, `Switch to Table View`, `Toggle Dark Mode`
      * Full arrow key (`↑`/`↓`) and `Enter` keyboard navigation.
    - UX Benefit: Power-user speed and premium modern app aesthetic.
    - Effort: ~70 lines in `js/app.js` + modal CSS.

3. FEATURE & UI PILLAR 2: DATA DENSITY & VIEW CUSTOMIZATION
================================================================================

3.1 Dual-Currency Toggle (USD $ vs. INR ₹ Primary Mode):
    - Context: Indian founders/angels think in Lakhs & Crores (₹ Cr), while global VCs evaluate in Millions ($M).
    - Actionable Solution:
      * Add a discrete segmented toggle in the header or toolbar: `[ USD $ | INR ₹ ]`.
      * In USD mode: Headline displays `$3.47M` with secondary `₹29 Cr`.
      * In INR mode: Headline displays `₹29 Cr` with secondary `$3.47M`.
      * Automatically persists user preference in `localStorage`.
    - UX Benefit: Tailored user experience for both domestic Indian and international venture audiences.
    - Effort: ~30 lines in `js/app.js`.

3.2 Card Density Switcher (Standard Cards vs. Compact Rows):
    - Context: Analysts scanning 100+ rounds prefer maximum information density per screen viewport.
    - Actionable Solution:
      * In the toolbar, add a density toggle: `[ Standard Cards | Compact Rows ]`.
      * Compact mode reduces vertical card padding, hides secondary descriptions until hover/click, and lines up key metadata in a single slim tile.
    - UX Benefit: Increases visible deals on screen by 2.5x on laptop and desktop displays.
    - Effort: ~35 lines in `css/styles.css` and ~10 lines in `js/app.js`.

3.3 Table View Column Visibility Customizer:
    - Context: Different analysts care about different metrics (e.g., founders vs. employee count vs. sources).
    - Actionable Solution:
      * In Table View mode, add a discrete "Columns ▾" popover allowing users to check/uncheck columns:
        [✓] Date  [✓] Company  [✓] Round  [✓] Amount  [✓] Investors  [ ] Founders  [ ] Employees  [ ] Year Founded  [✓] Sources
      * Save chosen column configurations in `localStorage`.
    - UX Benefit: Gives professional power users full spreadsheet-like flexibility.
    - Effort: ~45 lines in `js/app.js`.

3.4 Dynamic High-Resolution Company Logos (with Crisp Initials Fallback):
    - Context: Text-only cards lack visual anchoring and brand recognition.
    - Actionable Solution:
      * Render company brand avatars using Clearbit/Google Favicon/Unavatar API:
        `<img src="https://unavatar.io/${domain}?fallback=false" onerror="this.replaceWith(createInitialsSvg(name))" />`
      * For companies without icons, generate vibrant, deterministic CSS gradient initials (e.g., "MM" for MoroMaa on purple/violet mesh).
    - UI Benefit: Instantly elevates the visual finish to Crunchbase/ProductHunt standard.
    - Effort: ~25 lines in `js/app.js` and `css/styles.css`.

4. FEATURE & UX PILLAR 3: FOUNDER & VENTURE INTELLIGENCE TOOLS
================================================================================

4.1 1-Click Founder Social & Background Deep Links:
    - Context: When analyzing a funded company, investors and job seekers immediately look up the founders.
    - Actionable Solution:
      * On company pages and cards next to each Founder name, add micro action badges:
        [ 💼 Search LinkedIn ]  [ 🔍 Google Dossier ]  [ 🐦 Search X / Twitter ]
      * Clicking opens pre-targeted searches (e.g. `site:linkedin.com/in "Soundous Moufakir" "MoroMaa"`).
    - UX Benefit: Eliminates manual copying, pasting, and searching across multiple tabs.
    - Effort: ~20 lines in `js/app.js` and `scripts/build-deal-pages.js`.

4.2 "Similar Deals & Competitors" Recommender on Company Pages:
    - Context: Analysts want to know who else operates in the same space.
    - Actionable Solution:
      * At the bottom of each company dossier page (`/company/<domain>/`), automatically render 2 to 3 related companies in the same sector from the dataset:
        "Similar Companies in Beauty & Wellness / D2C"
        [ Card: DheyaTech • $2.5M Seed ] [ Card: The Skin Co • $1.2M Pre-Series A ]
      * Clicking any related card navigates directly to that company page.
    - UX Benefit: Keeps users exploring the platform longer and increases AdSense page impressions.
    - Effort: ~35 lines in `scripts/build-deal-pages.js`.

4.3 Local Watchlist & "Save Deal" Bookmarking (Private & Zero-Server):
    - Context: Users often want to save 5-10 deals to review during their weekly team meetings.
    - Actionable Solution:
      * Add a subtle bookmark icon (⭐ or 🔖) on every deal card and table row.
      * Add a "⭐ Saved Deals (3)" filter pill in the toolbar.
      * Persist bookmarked deal IDs in `localStorage`.
      * Allow 1-click export of saved deals as a private Markdown/CSV briefing.
    - UX Benefit: Transforms Fundingly from a passive reader into an active daily workflow tool.
    - Effort: ~45 lines in `js/app.js`.

4.4 Side-by-Side Company Comparison Matrix (Compare 2-4 Deals):
    - Context: Users frequently compare 2 competing startups in the same sector.
    - Actionable Solution:
      * Add a "Compare" checkbox on cards / table rows.
      * Selecting 2+ deals surfaces a floating bottom bar: `[ Compare 2 Companies ⚡ ]`.
      * Opens a side-by-side split comparison table (Founding Year, Valuation, Investors, Sector, City, Team Size).
    - UX Benefit: Incredible utility for investment memos and competitive landscape analysis.
    - Effort: ~65 lines in `js/app.js`.
