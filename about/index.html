About Fundingly.in

Indian Startup Funding Intelligence

Fundingly.in is a dedicated public intelligence platform tracking Indian startup investments, venture capital rounds, and market funding momentum in real-time.

Our Mission

The Indian startup ecosystem is growing at breakneck speed, with hundreds of seed, venture, and growth rounds announced every month across Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, Mumbai, and emerging hubs. Our mission is to make venture data open, accessible, transparent, and easy to explore for every founder, operator, angel investor, and student.

Key Platform Features

  • Weekly Grouped Deal Timelines: Track latest rounds chronologically with one-click expandable quarters and weeks.
  • Smart Multi-Dimensional Filters: Filter across funding stages, venture capital investors, headquarters, and sectors.
  • Direct Source Verification: Every deal links directly to verified news articles and press releases.
  • 100% Free & Open Access: No paywalls, mandatory logins, or gated reports.
