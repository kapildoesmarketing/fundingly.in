Founder's Note: The Story Behind Fundingly.in

Letter from the Creator By Kapil Pidhwani • September 2026

Hey there! I'm Kapil Pidhwani, the creator and builder of Fundingly.in.

If you’ve ever tried tracking Indian startup funding deals, venture rounds, and angel investments across the web, you've probably faced the same frustration I did: scattered press releases, messy spreadsheets, ad-cluttered news blogs, and expensive corporate database paywalls that lock away public market knowledge.

"I believe venture intelligence in India shouldn't be locked behind enterprise paywalls or buried under clickbait news. It should be fast, transparent, beautifully designed, and freely accessible to everyone building the future."

Why I Built Fundingly.in

India has the world's most vibrant startup ecosystem — from AI and DeepTech in Bengaluru to Fintech in Mumbai and QuickCommerce across Gurugram. Yet, discovering who raised what, which VC led the round, and which sectors are accelerating has always required navigating dozens of separate media portals.

I built Fundingly.in with a single guiding mission: to create the cleanest, fastest, and most delightful home for Indian startup funding data on the internet.

The Product Philosophy

Every pixel in Fundingly.in is crafted with care:

• Zero Paywalls & 100% Free: Knowledge belongs to founders, operators, students, and angels alike.
• Apple-Grade Craftsmanship: Subtle frosted glass, tactile haptic feedback, fluid swipe gestures, and OLED dark mode.
• Extreme Speed ($0 Server Architecture): Engineered on modern static delivery, loading millions in venture deals in under 300 milliseconds.
• Deep Interconnectivity: Filter by stage, sector, geography, or investment bracket with instantaneous in-memory search.

Join the Journey & Root for Us

Fundingly.in is an independent, founder-led project built in public. If this tool saves you time, helps you prepare for investor meetings, or inspires your next startup idea, that is the greatest reward.

I read and reply to every single message. If you have feedback, feature ideas, data corrections, or just want to chat about Indian tech, please reach out!
