2. HOW ADSENSE WORKS: "AUTO ADS" VS "MANUAL AD UNITS"
================================================================================
Google AdSense delivers ads in two ways:

1. AUTO ADS:
   Google's AI automatically scans your page and injects ads wherever it thinks
   fit (top banners, inside lists, vignettes/popups, bottom anchors).
   *Risk:* If left on defaults, Auto Ads might inject random ads on your Homepage.

2. MANUAL AD UNITS (Display Ads):
   You create specific ad slots in AdSense (with a unique data-ad-slot ID) and
   place them strictly where YOU want (e.g., sidebar and bottom of company pages).
   *Benefit:* 100% layout control, clean design, zero clutter on homepage.

3. STEP-BY-STEP: HOW TO PREVENT ADS ON HOMEPAGE BUT SHOW ON DEAL PAGES
================================================================================

Choose either Method A (simplest & most controlled) or Method B below:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD A (RECOMMENDED): TURN OFF AUTO ADS & USE MANUAL AD UNITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the gold standard for clean UI sites.

Step 1: Log in to Google AdSense (https://adsense.google.com).
Step 2: In the left sidebar, click "Ads" -> "By site".
Step 3: Find "fundingly.in" in your sites table and click the Pencil (Edit) icon.
Step 4: On the right side panel under "Ad settings":
        - Toggle "Auto ads" to OFF.
Step 5: Click "Apply to site" at the bottom right.

Result:
- Homepage: ZERO ads will ever show.
- Static Pages (About, Terms, Privacy, Founders Note): ZERO ads.
- Company Deal Pages: Only the exact sidebar and footer ad units you specify
  will display!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD B (ALTERNATIVE): KEEP AUTO ADS ON BUT EXCLUDE HOMEPAGE URL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Use this if you want Google's AI to auto-optimize ad placements on deal pages,
but strictly want the Homepage excluded.

Step 1: In Google AdSense dashboard, go to "Ads" -> "By site".
Step 2: Click the Pencil (Edit) icon next to "fundingly.in".
Step 3: In the right panel under "Ad settings", turn "Auto ads" ON.
Step 4: Scroll down to "Page exclusions" and click "Manage".
Step 5: Click "+ Add exclusion".
Step 6: Enter your homepage URLs:
        - https://fundingly.in/
        - https://fundingly.in/index.html
Step 7: Choose exclusion type: "This page only".
Step 8: Click "Add" and then "Apply to site".

Result:
- Homepage is completely blacklisted from Auto Ads.
- Google will only place Auto Ads on subpages (company deal pages).

5. CREATING DEDICATED AD UNITS (SIDEBAR & BOTTOM SLOTS)
================================================================================
Once your site is approved, replace the placeholder ad slots with your official
Display Ad units:

Step 1: In AdSense, go to "Ads" -> "By ad unit".
Step 2: Select "Display ads".
Step 3: Create two ad units:
   Unit 1: Name it "Fundingly Deal Sidebar"
           Ad size: Responsive (or Square/Vertical)
           Click "Create" and copy the `data-ad-slot` number (e.g., 1234567890).

   Unit 2: Name it "Fundingly Deal Bottom Banner"
           Ad size: Responsive (Horizontal)
           Click "Create" and copy the `data-ad-slot` number (e.g., 9876543210).

Step 4: Update `Repo Files/scripts/build-deal-pages.js`:
   - Open `build-deal-pages.js`
   - Look for the sidebar ad slot (approx line 1188) and bottom ad slot (approx line 1261).
   - Set `data-ad-slot="YOUR_SIDEBAR_SLOT_ID"` and `data-ad-slot="YOUR_BOTTOM_SLOT_ID"`.

Step 5: Regenerate pages by running in terminal:
   node scripts/build-deal-pages.js
   git add -A && git commit -m "feat: configure active ad slot IDs" && git push origin main

8. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON FAQS
================================================================================
Q1: Will having the <script> tag on Homepage cause ads to appear on Homepage?
A1: NO. The <script> tag in <head> only loads the AdSense JavaScript library.
    Ads will only render on the homepage if:
    a) You put an `<ins class="adsbygoogle">` tag on index.html (which we did NOT), OR
    b) Auto Ads is turned ON without a Homepage URL exclusion rule.
    Follow Method A (Auto Ads OFF) or Method B (Page Exclusion) to guarantee no ads on Homepage.

Q2: Why are blank spaces showing instead of ads initially?
A2: Until AdSense officially approves your domain, AdSense serves blank containers
    to prevent invalid impressions. Once approved, live targeted ads appear automatically.

Q3: Can I place ads inside the table or timeline on the homepage?
A3: It is recommended to keep the Homepage 100% ad-free as requested to provide
    the fastest, cleanest venture database experience. The subpages (individual
    company dossiers) provide higher time-on-page and higher ad CPC.
